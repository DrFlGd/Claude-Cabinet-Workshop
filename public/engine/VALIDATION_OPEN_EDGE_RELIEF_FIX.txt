Open-edge CNC relief fix validation
===================================

Issue
-----
Dogbone / T-bone corner relief was being generated at slot corners that lie on
an open panel perimeter. Those corners already have cutter access, so relief
there creates an unwanted scallop in the finished outside edge.

Core geometry change
--------------------
- slot_corner_relief_2d(), slot_shape_2d(), slot_cut_x_3d(), and
  slot_cut_z_3d() now accept open-edge flags for the slot's left/right/bottom/top
  walls.
- A dogbone/T-bone circle is emitted only when BOTH walls forming that corner
  are closed/internal.
- Existing callers remain backward compatible because all flags default false.

Open-edge cases now handled
---------------------------
- Stackable cabinet bottom-tab receivers located entirely in the raised front
  or rear stacking pads: bottom corners are open, so no bottom dogbone/T-bone.
- Cabinet top receiver slots: top corners are open at the panel perimeter.
- Stackable base front/rear receiver slots: relief is suppressed on the outward
  front/rear edge.
- Toe-kick receiver slots when toe_kick_setback == 0: front edge is open.
- Rear stretcher receiver slots when back_stretcher_inset == 0: rear edge is open.
- Ordinary fully enclosed slots still retain all required relief corners.

Stackable-specific behavior
---------------------------
The stack-safe bottom tab placement remains unchanged. For a bottom slot, the
bottom edge is treated as open only when the nominal tab lies wholly inside the
front or rear high stacking pad. The center bottom slot over the lower tongue is
still treated as an internal slot and keeps its CNC relief.

Compile validation
------------------
Successfully compiled to CSG with:
  joinery_style = "tab_slot"
  slot_corner_relief = "dogbone"

Frontends checked:
