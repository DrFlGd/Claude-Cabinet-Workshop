# Hardware library inherited by Modular Organization v3

> Historical engine documentation. Applies to the version named below, not the current application. See the [documentation guide](../../docs/README.md) and [consolidated changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).


Date researched: 2026-09-15

This catalog was expanded in the Modular Storage v35 parent and is carried forward by the fork with source-traceable manufacturer data from Blum, Hettich, Salice, GRASS, Accuride, and Knape & Vogt (KV).

## Side-mount slide families mapped to the inherited geometry

- Accuride 3832E — exact inch-length variants from 6–28 in, 12.7 mm side space, 45.7 mm height.
- Accuride 3634EC — 14–28 in Easy-Close variants, 19.8 mm side space, 53.1 mm height.
- Accuride 9301E — 10–36 in even lengths plus 40/42/44/48/54/60 in, 19.1 mm side space, 76.2 mm height.
- KV 8400 — 8–28 in current product-page variants, 12.7 mm side clearance, 45.5 mm height.
- KV 8450FM — 12–28 in soft-close variants, 12.7 mm side clearance, 45.5 mm height.
- Hettich KA 5632 — 250–700 mm family, 46 x 12.7 mm cross-section.

These profiles apply safe envelope dimensions but keep automatic mounting-hole drilling off when the rail provides multiple valid slots/holes.

## Hinges

- Blum CLIP top BLUMOTION 110° screw-on overlay/inset records use current 71B3550/71B3750 part numbers; dual/half-overlay 71B3650 is catalog-only.
- Hettich Sensys 8645i 9073605/9073606/9073607 records capture overlay/half-overlay/inset data and TH 52 x 5.5 mm drilling metadata.
- Salice Silentia+ Series 100 105° records capture full overlay, 1/2 in overlay, half overlay, and inset arm variants.
- GRASS Tiomos 110 records capture 35 mm cup, minimum cup depth, and 45 x 9.5 mm drilling metadata.
- Blum COMPACT BLUMOTION 38N/39C face-frame families are catalog-only until face-frame cabinet geometry is modeled.

The current inherited geometry does not yet encode hinge fixing-wing lateral offset, so manufacturer wing-hole patterns are metadata unless fully representable.

## Concealed/undermount runner records retained as metadata-only

- Blum MOVENTO 760H / 766H
- Blum TANDEM plus BLUMOTION 563H
- Hettich Quadro V6 Silent System 500 mm reference
- GRASS Dynapro 40 kg family
- Salice Futura A755

Their drawer-width formulas, installation depths, system lines, capacities, and nominal length ranges are retained without forcing them through the side-mount geometry model.

## Source policy

Only manufacturer pages/catalogs/technical sheets are embedded for the added records. The catalog retains source URL and retrieval date so the website can expose provenance and future refresh tools can detect stale records.
