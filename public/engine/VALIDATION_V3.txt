Validation/coverage boundaries
------------------------------
- OpenSCAD remains the authority for module geometry, interface records, semantic checks, and manufacturing features.
- modular_organization_validate.py / manufacturing export remains the exhaustive CUT/POCKET contour audit per OpenSCAD module.
- Project-level collision in v3 is conservative AABB-envelope checking, not mesh boolean collision.
- Automatic mating supports translation and orthogonal Z rotation only (0/90/180/270).
- General constraint solving, motion-envelope analysis, arbitrary 3-D mating, and structural/load analysis are not claimed in v3.
- Full STL rendering of the example assembly is intentionally optional and can be computationally slow under OpenSCAD 2021.01; it is not required for graph resolution or manufacturing validation.
